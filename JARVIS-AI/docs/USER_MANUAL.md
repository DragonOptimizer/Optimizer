# JARVIS: short user manual

**Talking.** Say “Hey Jarvis” (if the wake word works on your PC), press `Ctrl+Alt+J`, or click **Talk**. Speak; when you stop, JARVIS transcribes, thinks, acts, and answers aloud. Speak over it to interrupt. **Conversation** keeps listening after each reply.

**Watching it work.** The orb shows the real state (idle, listening, processing, performing action, awaiting authorization, error, offline). “What I'm doing” lists actual tool events: planned, running, completed, failed, denied. Nothing is shown unless it happened.

**Permissions.** Reading and reversible actions are automatic (Balanced) while sensitive ones pop up a dialog with the exact action: *Deny*, *Allow once*, or *Always allow this tool* (never offered for sensitive/dangerous). Switch to **Strict** under Computer.

**Examples**
* “Open Chrome, search for the latest AMD GPU drivers and show me the official download page.”
* “Create a folder called School Projects on my desktop and move these three documents into it.”
* “Check my unread email and summarise the important ones.” → “Draft a reply to my manager.” (drafts only; sending asks you)
* “What assignments are due this week?” → “Make a schedule and message it to my phone.”
* “Remind me at 7 PM to do my assignment, and call me.” (calls require the Phone setup)
* “Remember that I prefer short explanations.”
* “Take these clips, make a 30-second vertical montage, add subtitles, and message me when it's ready.”

**Phone.** Message your bot from Telegram. Remote requests run read-only; send `/stop` to halt everything.

**Stopping.** ■ STOP button, tray → EMERGENCY STOP, `Ctrl+Alt+Shift+X`, or `/stop` on Telegram. Resume under Privacy & security.

**Floating assistant.** Settings → Window & startup, or the Overlay button/tray: a small always-on-top orb with talk, stop, and expand buttons.
